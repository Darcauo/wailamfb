About Waila

A customer-focused microfinance bank serving Karu, Nasarawa.

Waila Microfinance Bank is a financial services institution serving communities in and around Karu, Nasarawa State, with a focus on inclusive banking, practical financial support, and long term customer relationships.

Publicly available information identifies the institution as focused on microfinance, SME lending, and agricultural finance, helping households, traders, and growing businesses access the financial tools they need to plan and progress.

We believe finance should be practical, human, and accessible.
